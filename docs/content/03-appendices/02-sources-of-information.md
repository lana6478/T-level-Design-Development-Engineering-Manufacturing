# Sources of Information

*Design and Development for Engineering and Manufacturing T Level → Appendices*

Beyond the specification itself, City & Guilds publishes a small set of documents that providers delivering this Technical Qualification are expected to refer to. They're aimed at course providers/centres rather than students directly, but are listed here for completeness - they're all downloadable from [cityandguilds.com](https://www.cityandguilds.com).

## Centre and quality assurance documents

- **Centre Contract General Terms**
- **Quality Assurance Standards: Centre Handbook**
- **Quality Assurance Standards: Centre Assessment**

Between them, these cover:

- centre assessment;
- internal quality assurance (IQA) and IQA strategy;
- alternative locations and subcontractors;
- non-compliance and malpractice;
- centre support roles and resources.

All T Level providers must familiarise themselves with these documents and adhere to the general terms as part of their conditions of approval.

## Key terms

- **IQA (Internal Quality Assurance)** - a centre's own process for checking that assessment and marking meet the required standard before results go to City & Guilds.
- **Centre** - the school, college or training provider approved to deliver the qualification (referred to as a "provider" elsewhere in this guide).
