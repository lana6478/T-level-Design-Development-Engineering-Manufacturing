# Sources and Further Reading

*Design and Development for Engineering and Manufacturing T Level → Sources and Further Reading*

This whole content library is a study-guide summary derived from the official specification for the T Level Technical Qualification in Design and Development for Engineering and Manufacturing (Level 3), Mechanical Engineering pathway. It's meant to make the qualification's content easier to browse and revise from - but for anything assessment-critical (exact wording, mark allocations, deadlines), always check the primary source documents below.

## Primary sources

- [T Level Technical Qualification in Design and Development for Engineering and Manufacturing - Specification v1.5 (City & Guilds, official spec PDF)](https://www.cityandguilds.com/-/media/productdocuments/engineering/mechanical/8714/centre_documents/dd-t-level-technical-qualification-specification-v15-pdf.pdf)
- [City & Guilds T Level qualification page (8714)](https://www.cityandguilds.com/qualifications-and-apprenticeships/engineering/mechanical/8714-t-level-technical-qualification-in-design-and-development-for-engineering-and-manufacturing)
- [Institute for Apprenticeships and Technical Education (IfATE) - T Level outline content and occupational standards](https://www.instituteforapprenticeships.org/)
- [T Levels official government information site](https://www.tlevels.gov.uk/)

## Other specialism pathways (not covered here)

This qualification also offers three other occupational specialism pathways, which this study guide does **not** cover: Electrical and Electronic Engineering, Control and Instrumentation Engineering, and Structural Engineering. If you're studying one of those instead of Mechanical Engineering, the official specification above covers all four.

## About this library

This is an **independent, unofficial study-guide repository**, part of the [UTC Leeds T Level Study Guides](https://lana6478.github.io/utc-leeds-t-levels/) project. It organises and paraphrases the official T Level specification into smaller, individually-named files and a searchable wiki-style site, to make the qualification's content easier to navigate - it is not a substitute for the specification itself and isn't produced or endorsed by City & Guilds or IfATE. It was built from **Version 1.5 (March 2026)** of the specification; if a later version is published, check the primary source above for anything that may have changed.
