# Qualification Structure and Assessment

*Design and Development for Engineering and Manufacturing T Level → Overview → Structure*

This page summarises the qualification "at a glance" - its official numbers, requirements and assessment methods - plus how the Technical Qualification (TQ) is actually built from its two components.

## Technical qualification structure

The TQ is made up of **two mandatory components**, both of which must be achieved to gain the full T Level Technical Qualification:

1. **The common core component (300 - Engineering common core)** - Level 3, 680 GLH, 1000 TQT. This gives learners a broad base of knowledge and skills that applies across the engineering industry and every Occupational Specialism linked to this T Level, while also developing the core skills they'll need on the job.
2. **One standalone Occupational Specialism, chosen from:**

| Component code | Unit | Occupational Specialism | Level | GLH | TQT |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8714-31 | 321 | **Mechanical engineering** (covered by this guide) | 3 | 680 | 1000 |
| 8714-32 | 322 | Electrical and electronic engineering | 3 | 680 | 1000 |
| 8714-33 | 323 | Control and instrumentation engineering | 3 | 680 | 1000 |
| 8714-34 | 324 | Structural engineering | 3 | 680 | 1000 |

Occupational Specialisms develop the knowledge, skills and behaviours needed to reach **threshold competence** in an occupation - meaning a learner's attainment is of a standard to enter that occupation and industry, with the expectation they'll build up to full occupational competence over time with proper support and training.

This guide only covers the **Mechanical engineering** specialism (unit 321 / 8714-31), since that is the pathway taught at UTC Leeds - it does not cover units 322, 323 or 324.

## What the key terms mean

- **Guided Learning Hours (GLH)** - the average number of hours of direct delivery a unit requires (contact with tutors/trainers, formal learning such as classes and tutorials, and time spent preparing for and completing assessment), rounded up to the nearest five hours.
- **Total Qualification Time (TQT)** - the total time a learner is expected to spend achieving the qualification, including GLH plus independent preparation and study time.
- **Criteria and Range** - criteria set out the subject/topic to be delivered and assessed; the accompanying Range spells out exactly what must be covered under that topic.

## Key terms

- **GLH** - Guided Learning Hours, the directly-delivered teaching time for a unit.
- **TQT** - Total Qualification Time, GLH plus independent study time.
- **Threshold competence** - attainment sufficient to enter an occupation, with full competence expected to develop on the job.
- **Common core** - the shared unit (300) every learner studies regardless of specialism.
